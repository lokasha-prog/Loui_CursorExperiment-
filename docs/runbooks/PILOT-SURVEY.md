# Pilot survey (send to staff and parents after two weeks; English and Arabic versions side by side)

Use a simple form tool the nursery already has. Keep it under 3 minutes.

1. How likely are you to recommend this system to another nursery or a friend? (0 to 10)  `NPS: promoters 9-10, detractors 0-6`
2. Would you recommend it? Yes / No / Not sure
3. Which task is easiest now? (check-in, photos and updates, messages, leave, reports, other)
4. Which task is still hard or slow? (free text)
5. Did anything go wrong or confuse you? (free text, mention the screen)
6. Language you used: English / Arabic. Was every screen readable and in the right direction? Yes / No (which?)
7. Staff only: how much time does the system save or cost you each day? (saves more than 30 min / saves a little / same / costs a little / costs more than 30 min)
8. Parents only: how often did you open the daily feed? (every day / a few times a week / rarely / never) and did you read the daily report? 
9. One thing we should build next.

## Retrospective (end of week 12, 1 hour, owner + pilot manager + team)
- What went well, what hurt, what we will change.
- Numbers: staff and parents active, attendance coverage, open bugs by severity, alert delivery time, NPS.
- Decisions for Sprint 6: top 5 fixes, top 3 features, anything blocking the next nursery.

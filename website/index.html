A day with us

A gentle rhythm of play, rest and discovery

  1. Warm welcome
    Children are greeted by name and settle in with free play.
  2. Circle time and stories
    Songs, languages and storytelling to start the day together.
  3. Play and learning
    Indoor and outdoor activities for movement, creativity and early skills.
  4. Healthy meals and rest
    Nutritious food that respects every family's needs, then a quiet rest.
  5. Home with a daily report
    You hear how the day went, with photos and notes, before you arrive.

Questions

Things families often ask

Can I visit before I decide?

Yes. Send us the form below and we will arrange a tour at a time that suits you.

What ages do you take?

Tell us your child's date of birth in the form and we will explain which group is right.

How do I know my child is safe?

Children are checked in and out securely, only named people may collect them, and we keep staff cover in every room.

What about fees?

Fees depend on the programme and days. We will send a clear breakdown when you enquire.

Safety and wellbeing

Peace of mind, built into every day

You should never have to wonder whether your child is safe. These are the things we do, not just promise.

  • Secure check-in and check-out with a scanned card, recorded every time
  • Only the people you name may collect your child, with identity checked
  • Staff-to-child ratios watched live, so every room always has enough adults
  • Allergies and medical needs recorded and visible to the team, even with no signal
  • Photos and details shared only with your own family, under UAE data-protection rules

Questions

Things families often ask

Can I visit before I decide?

Yes, please do. Send us the form below and we will arrange a tour at a time that suits you.

What ages do you take?

See our programmes above. Tell us your child's date of birth in the form and we will explain which group is right.

How do I know my child is safe?

Children are checked in and out securely, only named people may collect them, and we keep the right number of adults in every room.

What about fees?

Fees depend on the programme and days. We will send a clear breakdown when you enquire, with nothing hidden.

What if my child has allergies or special needs?

Tell us everything during your visit. Allergies, medical needs and routines are recorded and followed by the whole team.

Do you speak Arabic and English with the children?

Yes. Both languages are part of the day, in songs, stories and conversation.

How will I hear about my child's day?

Through our parent app: photos, meals, naps and a daily report, plus messages with the team.
